# Buyer notes — NoticeCliff

Signature: Mourad.Soltani

One-to-one notes only. Do not upload these into a mail merge or bulk sender.

## Who it is for

Finance or ops people at 10–80 person companies who still track SaaS renewals in a sheet. They already feel the pain of a 30-day notice clause. They are not looking for a full SaaS management suite.

## What they buy

A local calculator and a JSON endpoint they can drop next to an existing sheet. No vendor login, no agent that cancels contracts, no legal opinion.

## Pricing sketch

- Free local CLI for a single contract.
- $19/month if you later host a private workspace with saved vendors.
- Do not promise holiday calendars or certified-mail tracking in v1.

## Honest limits to say out loud

- Weekend skipping is included. Public holidays are only skipped if the buyer passes dates.
- Notice method (email vs letter) is the buyer's problem.
- This does not read contracts or PDFs.

## Fit check before a call

Ask whether they have at least five auto-renewing tools with a written notice window. If they have one credit card and no contracts, this is not the product.
