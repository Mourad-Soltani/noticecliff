## After they ask how the date is calculated

Subject: How NoticeCliff counts the cancel-by date

Hi {first_name},

NoticeCliff treats the renewal date as already too late. It starts the day before renewal and walks back your notice window. Calendar mode counts every day. Business mode skips Saturday and Sunday. A buffer day is just extra lead time for posting a letter.

On a 1 Dec 2026 renewal with 30 calendar days of notice, the cancel-by date is 1 Nov 2026. I can run your actual date the same way if you paste renewal date, notice days, and whether the clause says business days.

Mourad.Soltani

## After they ask what it does not do

Subject: What NoticeCliff will not do

Hi {first_name},

It will not email the vendor, cancel the seat, or read the contract PDF. It only returns the last date a notice can still land, plus a risk band from the date you pass in. If the clause needs a holiday calendar, you pass those dates in. I would rather under-promise that than hide it.
